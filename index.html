About

I am interested in computational problems that begin with real-world needs and open up room for methodological, algorithmic, or theoretical contributions.

My undergraduate training combined mathematics and finance. Through projects in production decision-making, supply-chain risk measurement, and social-network analysis, I worked with statistical inference, optimization, dynamic programming, natural language processing, and network simulation.

I am currently reading across different areas and learning how research questions are formulated. Rather than committing prematurely to a particular technique or field, I hope to identify problems that are useful in practice and rewarding to study in depth.

Production decision-making under inspection uncertainty

A multi-stage model for production systems with inspection costs and uncertain quality. I led the team, developed the model, and used sequential probability ratio testing, dynamic programming, and sensitivity analysis to study robust decisions.

Influence maximization in social networks
